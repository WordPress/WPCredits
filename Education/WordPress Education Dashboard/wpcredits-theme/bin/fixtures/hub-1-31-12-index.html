Choosing a course

Which WordPress Credits course?

What each asks of a student, so a school and its students can choose. Access to each course comes through the student's institution.

  • Intro to Contribution →

    50 hours

    One guided contribution project: a first look at open source, for a shorter course or an elective.

  • Foundations →

    150 hours or more

    Your own site and a contribution that ships, for an internship, a capstone, or a full semester.

  • Developer track →

    For developers

    WordPress developer training and patch testing practice, toward a contribution that ships.

  • Designer track →

    For designers

    A portfolio, Figma and the Site Editor's design tools, and a block pattern of your own.

  • Translation track →

    For translators

    Translation skills and real work with the Polyglots team: translate.wordpress.org and GlotPress, locale glossaries, software strings, and Learn WordPress lessons.
