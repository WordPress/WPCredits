## Posts, and how they are approved

From the Backer tier up, your company can write posts for the program's students, as the program's partner page says: a Backer one self-written educational post about the sponsored product, and a Champion or a Partner a second, with factual, value-neutral information about additional products. Posts are written in the site's editor. The *Posts* card on the Sponsor Dashboard has the *Write a post* button and lists every post your company wrote, with where each one stands.

**Writing.** A Backer has one post at a time, in review or published; a Champion or a Partner has two. Press *Write a post*, write in the editor, and submit the post for review. A post carries your company's byline and lands in your company's category, so readers know who wrote it. A post can carry images and links; it cannot carry code that runs on the page. When your tier includes no more posts right now, the *Posts* card says so where *Write a post* would be, and a post submitted anyway stays a draft. When your tier includes no posts at all, as at Friend and Supporter, the *Posts* card is on your Sponsor Dashboard only while your company has a post of its own (one from before the levels, one returned to you, or one the program held back), and says the same there, with the link to your posts in wp-admin, where you can still read them, edit a draft or a returned post and delete one. A post the program took off the site because your tier no longer includes it is **dormant**: the newest posts go first, the card says why, and a dormant post returns by itself when an Administrator sets a tier that includes it. If you edit a dormant post, it becomes a new submission, which an Administrator reviews before it is published again.

**Review.** Every post is read by an Administrator before it is published. The Administrator can **publish** it, or **return** it with a note that says what to change; a returned post comes back to you with the note on it, and you can submit it again. A published post appears to students and mentors under *Guides from your company* on your offer's card in their Tools section, and in the site's Sponsors category, which only signed-in participants can read.

**Rules.** Posts are for the program's participants: useful to a student learning WordPress, honest about what your product does, free of pricing pressure. The program's licensing and trademark rules apply to what a post says and links to. A post that reads as an advertisement is returned.

If posting is switched off for your company, the *Posts* card says so; ask your program contact.
