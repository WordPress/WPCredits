### When Need help? cannot help

Some questions the guides do not answer. When **Need help?** finds nothing, the box says so and gives you **Send to my mentor**. When it does answer but you are still stuck, a small link under the sources says *Still stuck? Send this question to your mentor.*

Press it and a form opens with the heading **Send this to your mentor**. It shows your question. Under it, **Anything your mentor should know?** takes a line of your own, up to 600 characters. You can leave it empty. Press **Send**.

You see *Sent. Your mentor gets an email and can reply to you directly.* Your mentor gets your question by email, with the guide pages **Need help?** showed you, and answers by replying. The reply comes to your own email address. Your question also waits on your mentor's Mentor Report Card until they mark it as answered.

### How many questions you can send

You can send your mentor three questions a day. A fourth gets *You have sent your mentor three questions today. Ask in the WordPress Credits Slack channel, or try again tomorrow.*

You cannot send the same question twice in a day. The answer then is *You sent your mentor this question in the last day. They will reply by email.*

### If you have no mentor

**Send to my mentor** shows only while you have a mentor and your mentoring has not finished. Without one, the box says *Still stuck? Ask in the WordPress Credits Slack channel.* and links the channel.

Do not type anything confidential in a question: it goes to your mentor by email.
