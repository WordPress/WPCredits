## Your details, your representatives and your agreement

**Your institution**, in the menu, opens with "What the program has on record about your institution, and who can see this page." It holds two cards, in this order: **Your institution's details** and **Representatives and tutors**. A tutor is shown only **Representatives and tutors**. Your Collaboration Agreement is a view of its own, **Agreement**. **Your institution** is in your menu once your agreement is accepted; before that, **Agreement** is where the work is, and you set your institution's name on the agreement document itself, as *Before your agreement is accepted* describes.

### Your institution's details

The card lists eleven details under two small headings, then the tracks your institution offers. A detail the program holds nothing for reads **Not given yet**.

- **Contact and agreement**: **Contact person**, **Their role**, **Signs the agreement**, **Their title**, **Students study in** and **Page about WordPress Credits**.
- **Name and address**: **Name**, **Website**, **City**, **Country** and **Contact's email**.

Every representative of your institution reads the card and may correct its details; a tutor is not shown it. The two headings differ in one way. The contact and agreement details are yours to correct at once. The name and address details are asked for, and an Administrator decides. The program reaches your institution by the contact's email, files it by its country and shows its name on a public page, so a person at the program checks a change to any of them first.

**Tracks offered** lists the tracks your institution offers. The program sets them, not you: to change them, write to the program. When your institution offers two or more, its students are asked which track they want when they register.

Under each list is a closed section, named with what a save does, that opens the form for that list alone. The form asks the questions your institution answered when it applied, in the same words and with the same rules, and shows the value the program has: a box for a name, a web address or an email address, a list of ticks for the languages, and for the country one field that takes typing and narrows its list ("Type to find your country").

### Correcting the contact and agreement details

Open **Correct these** (saved at once) under the first list, change what is wrong and press **Save the details**. The contact person is written to the program's records and the other details are kept on this site, straight away, the Administrators are emailed what changed and from what to what, and a line is added to your institution's history. The page says "Your institution's details are saved."

If a value is refused, the page says "Nothing was saved. Correct what is marked below and save again." and puts the reason under the box that has one; what you typed stays in the form. If the program's records cannot be written just now, it says "The program records could not be updated, so nothing was saved. Try again in a while." and nothing is saved here either, so the form and the records cannot disagree. If this site cannot keep the details that live on it, it says "The details could not be kept on the site, so nothing was saved. Try again in a while." A form sent with nothing changed says "Nothing was changed." and sends no email, and so does the same change sent again from another tab, or by a colleague, a moment after the first was saved. If a save of your institution's details is being made at that very moment, the page says "Someone else is saving your institution's details at this moment, so nothing was done. Try again in a moment." and what you typed stays in the form. A website or a page address with a space in it, or whose host has no dot, is refused the same way, under "Enter a web address such as example.com.".

### Asking to correct the name and address

Open **Ask to correct these** (an Administrator checks first) under the second list, change what is wrong and press **Send to an Administrator**. Nothing is written yet. The page says "Sent: an Administrator checks the change, and you will see it here once it is decided." and the Administrators are emailed that a request is waiting. A refused value stays in the form with its reason, and the page says "Nothing was sent. Correct what is marked below and send it again." A form with nothing changed says "Nothing was changed." If your institution's details are being saved, or a colleague's request sent, at that very moment, the page says "Someone else is saving your institution's details at this moment, so nothing was done. Try again in a moment.", nothing is sent, and what you typed stays in the form.

An Administrator approves the request or declines it, as a whole and never a detail at a time. On approval the program's records are written with what you asked for, and the account that asked is emailed what changed. On a decline the Administrator writes a note for you, and the account that asked is emailed the note.

Your institution has one request at a time. While one waits, the second list offers no form, and a colleague who asks meanwhile, from another account or another browser tab, is told "One change is already waiting for an Administrator." and nothing is made.

### While a request waits, and after it is decided

Under each row the request would change, the card says "Waiting for an Administrator:" with what was asked and the day it was asked, and **Withdraw it**. Any representative of your institution can press it, not only the account that asked; it takes the whole request back, says "The request is withdrawn." and tells the Administrators. If an Administrator decided the request a moment earlier, the page says so and nothing changes, and if a colleague, or another tab of yours, withdrew it first, the page says "Nothing was changed: that request had already been withdrawn."

Once the request is decided, every representative of your institution reads what became of it under the second list, until the next request is sent. After an approval the card says "Updated on" and the day. After a decline it says "An Administrator did not make the change you asked for:" and the note. After a withdrawal it says nothing.

### Your representatives

**Representatives and tutors** lists the people who can sign in for your institution, with how many there are in its title. Each person has a row: the name, with a tag, **the institution's contact**, on the account whose email address is the one the program writes to, and under it the email address, whether they are a **Representative** or a **Tutor**, and how their access came about and since when ("Added by an Administrator", "Joined by invitation" and so on). When the program's records name a contact who has no account here yet, that person is listed too, with the sentence "Named in the program records, with no account on this site yet. An Administrator can add one so they can see this page.", and is not counted in the title, since they cannot sign in yet. When that address belongs to an account that was removed from your institution, the sentence is "Named in the program records. Their account is no longer among this institution's people. An Administrator can add them again." A tutor reads only "Named in the program records.", and so does everyone when that account now belongs to another institution. Everyone with an account can see your students. Representatives can remove anyone, themselves included. A tutor sees the students, their Student Report Cards and the semester reports, and changes nothing, so a tutor is shown no **Remove** and no **Leave**, no invitations waiting and no invitation form.

- **Remove** takes a colleague's access away, and **Leave** on your own row takes yours. The page asks you to confirm first. The account is kept and nobody at the institution is emailed; when no one would be left, the Administrators are told. The program goes on writing to the contact's address until an Administrator changes it. To change who the program writes to, correct the **Contact person** with **Correct these** and ask to correct the **Contact's email** with **Ask to correct these**.
- **Invitations waiting** lists the invitations not yet accepted, each with who sent it, when, and the day it stops working. **Resend** sends a new link, and the link in the earlier email stops working; **Cancel invitation** asks first, then stops the link at once.
- **Invite a colleague** is a closed section. Open it, type the address in **Email address**, choose their **Level** and press **Send invitation**. They get one email with a link that is good for fourteen days. As the **Level**, **Representative** adds and changes students, invites colleagues and corrects the details; **Tutor** sees the students, their Student Report Cards and the semester reports, and changes nothing. Whoever accepts it sees the same students you do, and a representative can remove anybody, including you. Up to ten invitations can be waiting at a time, and each account can send five a day.
- **Tutors** is a list of the people who tutor your students, with a name and an email address each. It is the list the **Tutor** box offers when you enroll a student. A representative or an Administrator can open **Add a tutor**, give the **Name** and the **Email** and press **Add**, and can **Remove** one. Adding a tutor sends no email and makes no account, and **Remove** asks first. Removing a tutor takes the name off the list and leaves the person's account, if they have one, and the students already assigned to them as they are. A tutor who accepts a **Tutor** invitation is added to the list. The same name with two addresses is two entries, and the box offers the name once. A tutor sees the list and cannot change it.

A person who follows an invitation sees a page that asks "Accept this invitation?" and nothing happens until they press **Accept invitation**. If they have no account yet one is made for them, and they get an email with a link to set a password. The invitation works for the address it was sent to. An Administrator can also add an account for a colleague; ask your contact at the program.

### Your Collaboration Agreement

Once it is accepted, **Agreement** opens under the name **Collaboration Agreement**, with "The agreement between your institution and the program, and the signed copy on file." It is tagged **Accepted**, with the day it was accepted, and says how: "Signed and accepted through this site.", or that it was recorded from the program's own records or by an Administrator from the copy the program holds. **Download the accepted agreement** gives you the file when this site holds one; an agreement signed before this site existed is a copy the program holds itself, and there is no file to download. A tutor sees the **Accepted** tag, the day and how it was signed, with no download and no form.

Under it is the form to replace it, which begins "Replace it by uploading a newer signed copy. The agreement above stays in force until an Administrator accepts the new one." It is the same upload form as before your agreement was accepted, with **Choose a file** for the PDF. While a newer copy waits, the page says "A newer signed agreement is waiting for review. The agreement above stays in force until an Administrator accepts it.", with **Download the copy waiting for review** and **Withdraw it**.

If an Administrator revokes the agreement, everybody at your institution is emailed the reason. A representative's menu goes back to **Overview** and **Agreement**, with the reason on **Agreement** and a form to upload a new signed agreement: *Before your agreement is accepted* has the rest. A tutor cannot open the page until a new agreement is accepted.
