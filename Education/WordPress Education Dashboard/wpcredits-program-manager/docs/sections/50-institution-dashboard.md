## Your Institution Dashboard

Your institution's page on the Education Directory is the Institution Dashboard, at /institution-dashboard/. You reach it after signing in with the account the program made for you, or the one made for you when you accepted a colleague's invitation; everybody the program attached to your institution shares this one page, though a tutor sees less of it. A card above the page may invite you to set up a code for signing in, or tell you the program asks for one: *Signing in*, at the start of this guide, covers it.

What you read about your students comes from the program's records, which this site reads at intervals and not each time you open the page. A line says when: "Read from the program records on" a date, and how long ago that was. It sits under the figures on the **Overview** and at the foot of the roster on **Students**. A change an Administrator makes in the program's records can take a while to reach the page.

### The menu

The page is drawn in a frame. A bar across the top holds the site's name, **Homepage**, and your photo and first name, with **Log out** behind them. A menu runs down the side, and the view you are reading sits beside it. Each item in the menu is a view of its own, at its own address, in this order:

- **Overview**, where the page opens.
- **Students**: your roster, and a student's card.
- **Enroll students**, once the program has switched enrollment on for institutions.
- **Semester reports**: the reports the program prepares for your institution.
- **Your institution**: the cards **Your institution's details** and **Representatives and tutors**. A tutor sees **Representatives and tutors** alone.
- **Agreement**: your Collaboration Agreement.
- **Events**, while there is an event from the program's sponsors, or from the program, to join.
- **Program updates**, while the program has published an update for institutions. It opens the newest in the same frame, with the other updates listed beside it when there are more.

A tutor's menu has no **Enroll students** and no **Events**. Until your Collaboration Agreement is accepted, a representative's menu holds only **Overview** and **Agreement**, and **Program updates** when there is one. A tutor cannot open the page until then. *Before your agreement is accepted*, below, says what a representative does.

Under **Help** come:

- the Ask box, "Ask about the program", whenever the program has switched its question assistant on for institutions. It answers questions about the program and about WordPress from the WordPress documentation, and links back to the pages its answers came from. Your question is sent to an outside AI service, so do not type anything confidential.
- **Institution guide**, which opens this guide.
- **#wpcredits on Slack**, which opens the WordPress Credits Slack channel.
- **Write to the program**, which opens a mail to the Administrator your institution is assigned to, once the program has assigned one and has an address for them.

Beside a view's name, a red tag counts what waits for you in it, for example "1 waits". Beside **Overview**, the tag counts everything that waits. *Waiting for you*, below, says what is counted.

Each view but the Overview opens with its name and one sentence that says what it holds. **Agreement** opens under the name **Collaboration Agreement**. A view has no link back of its own: the menu is the way back. **Students** leads from a student's card back to the roster, with the filters you had set, and **Semester reports** leads from a report back to the list.

A view whose cards have nothing to show says "Nothing to show here yet." When you press a button, what happened is said on the same view: a form that could not be sent says why above that form, a button on a row of a list says what happened above the list's rows, and anything else is said at the top of its card.

On a narrower screen the menu shows its icons alone, and on a phone it opens as a drawer from the **Menu** button at the top and closes with **Close menu**. On a wide screen, **Collapse menu** at its foot folds it to its icons, and your browser remembers that.

### The Overview

The Overview opens with your institution's name, with the icon of your institution's own website beside it when that website has one. Under the name are the city and country, the website as a link, and "Contact:" with the name of the person the program writes to. They are what the program has on record for your institution. A representative corrects them on the card **Your institution's details**, on **Your institution**: *Your details, your representatives and your agreement* covers it. **Enroll students**, beside them, opens the **Enroll students** view while it is in your menu.

Next comes a row of figures for the semester the **Students** view opens on: how many students signed up, how many graduated, how many are waiting to graduate, how many are on the program now and how many are waiting for a mentor. Since 1.123.98 a student waiting to graduate has a figure of their own, and "on the program now" and "waiting for a mentor" no longer count them, so the figures after "signed up" never add up to more than it. A figure with nothing to count is left out, and with nothing at all to count there is no row of figures and no line under it.

#### Waiting for you

**Waiting for you** has one row for each thing that waits for you to act. Each row has a red "Waits" tag, says what waits, gives one sentence about it, and ends with a button that opens the view and the card that hold it:

- *Collaboration Agreement not signed yet*, *Collaboration Agreement returned* or *Collaboration Agreement revoked*, with **Open the agreement**, while your agreement waits for your institution. A signed copy you uploaded waits for an Administrator, not for you, so it is no row.
- *A checked list of students waits to be created*, with **Open the list**, while a list you checked on **Enroll students** has not been created.
When nothing waits, the Overview says "Nothing waits for you." The tag says "Waits" in words, so the red is never the only sign.

#### Good to know

**Good to know** follows when there is something to say that waits for nobody. Its rows are counted nowhere.

- *A list of students is being created*, tagged "In progress", while the list is being created. It carries on by itself.
- *Your semester report for* a semester *is ready*, with "Approved on" and the day, and **Read the report**, for thirty days after an Administrator approves it.
- *Your request was decided*, with what became of your request to correct your institution's name and address, and **See your details**, for thirty days after it was decided and until the next request is sent.
A row tagged "New" is news. It waits for nothing, and nothing has to be done about it.

Since 1.123.98 four facts about your students follow, each with no tag and **See them**, which lists the students it counts. They are counted nowhere, and each is there only while it counts at least one student. They count your students of every semester, where the figures above count the one semester named under them, so a fact and a figure with the same words can differ:

- *2 students have not signed in yet*: students on the program now or waiting for a mentor, whose account here was made since the site began recording sign-ins (the day a student's card names) and who have no sign-in on record. Accounts made before that day are not counted, because the site kept no sign-in record before then. "Open a student to send them an invitation." The page says only whether a sign-in is on record, never when. A tutor is not shown it.
- *2 students finish within 30 days*: students on the program now or waiting for a mentor whose end date is today or in the next 30 days.
- *1 placement ended and is still open*: students on the program now or waiting for a mentor whose end date has passed. "The end date has passed and the student is still on the program. Correct the date if the placement goes on." On the student's card, correct the date under **Correct this student's details**, or record how the placement ended under **Finishing this placement**. A graduation the card does not offer, or a student with no account, goes through your contact at the program. A tutor is not shown it.
- *1 student is waiting to graduate*: "The program is checking their work." and, when the site knows it, the day the wait began. There is nothing for you to do.

#### What your students work on

**What your students work on** (since 1.123.98) follows when there is something to say: a table of the teams the students of the semester the **Students** view opens on contribute to, with how many students are on each, most first, counted as the semester report counts its Contribution Teams; then one line that says how many different mentors your students on the program now have and, of that semester's students, for how many hours are on record. A student on two teams counts for each, and a student counts for hours when more than zero hours are on record. The page does not say why a student has no hours on record. The teams are counted from today's roster; a semester report keeps the numbers of the day it was made and takes each student's team from its own records, so the two can differ.

#### Your contact at the program

**Your contact at the program** ends the Overview: the Administrator your institution is assigned to, with a link to write to them when the program has an address for them, and **Book a call** when it has a booking link. It is there once an Administrator is assigned to your institution. It says "They look after your institution. Write to them about anything the guide does not answer."

### Before your agreement is accepted

Until an Administrator has accepted a signed Collaboration Agreement for your institution, or recorded one the program already holds, a representative's menu holds **Overview** and **Agreement**, and any other address of the page opens the Overview. A tutor is shown no page until then. The Overview has your institution's name and your contact at the program. While the agreement waits for your institution, *Waiting for you* has a row with **Open the agreement**. While a signed copy waits for review, or the program's records do not agree yet, it says "Nothing waits for you." **Agreement** opens under the name **Collaboration Agreement**, and what it says depends on where your agreement stands.

#### No agreement recorded yet

With no agreement recorded, **Agreement** says "The program has no Collaboration Agreement recorded for your institution. Until it does, its representatives reach only the Overview and the Agreement, and its tutors no page." Then it gives three steps.

1. Get the agreement. **Generate the agreement** makes the program's own template with the name you type in **The institution's name** ("Exactly as it should print on the agreement."). When the template exists in more than one language, choose one in **Language**. The document opens as a page your browser can save as a PDF or print. Nothing is sent to the program yet. You may use an agreement of your own instead; an Administrator reads it in full, so it takes longer.
2. Have it signed by somebody who can commit your institution.
3. Upload the signed PDF. Choose "The program's template, signed" or "An agreement of our own, signed". Tick the box that says it is signed by somebody who can commit the institution. Pick the file with **Choose a file** and press **Upload the signed agreement**. The file must be a PDF without a password.

Under the steps the page says that an institution that already signed an agreement with the program need not sign again: an Administrator can record the copy the program holds, and your account opens when they do. If that is you, write to your contact at the program.

#### The other states

- *Generated.* The page says on what day you generated the program's agreement and which template version it was, and that it is not signed and uploaded yet. **Open that document again**, in the first step, gives you the same document, with the name it was made with, and the three steps and the sentence about an agreement already signed stay as they were.
- *Waiting for review.* Tagged **Waiting for review**, the page says "We received your signed agreement on" a date, and that an Administrator will review it and you will get an email either way. **Download the copy we hold** gives you the file back, and **Withdraw it** deletes the file from this site so that you can upload another.
- *Returned.* Tagged **Returned**, the page says "An Administrator returned your agreement, with this note:" and shows the note, with who left it and on which day, and a form to upload the corrected agreement, signed, as a PDF.
- *Revoked.* Tagged **Revoked**, the page says the program revoked the agreement, and that until a new agreement is accepted its representatives reach only the Overview and the Agreement, and its tutors no page. It shows the note the program left, with a form to upload a new signed agreement. An Administrator reviews it the same way as the first one.
- *Accepted, but not yet agreed everywhere.* If this site holds an accepted agreement and the program's own record does not agree with it yet, the page says an Administrator can see the difference and settles it. There is nothing for you to do.

When an Administrator accepts the agreement, everybody at your institution is emailed, a representative's whole menu opens, and a tutor can open the page. **Agreement** then shows the accepted agreement: *Your Collaboration Agreement* covers it.

### Events from our sponsors

**Events** lists the events the program's sponsors host for its students, mentors, Administrators and institutions, when there are any to join. It is in a representative's menu, and not in a tutor's. The list follows the view's name and its sentence, with no heading of its own. Each event shows its host, its day and time in your own time zone, how long it runs and how many places are left, with **Join**. Once you are on an event the page says "You are on it.", and shows **Leave**, which gives your place back, and **Join the call** when the event has a call link. The email you get when you join carries the call link and a calendar file.
