## Your Institution Dashboard

Your institution's page on the Education Directory is the Institution Dashboard, at /institution-dashboard/. You reach it after signing in with the account the program made for you, or the one made for you when you accepted a colleague's invitation; every person the program attached to your institution sees the same page. A card above the page may invite you to set up a code for signing in, or tell you the program asks for one: *Signing in*, at the start of this guide, covers it.

The page opens with your institution's name and the places to go with a question, and then comes the work, in three sections: **Students**, **Semester report** and **Representatives and agreement**. Until your Collaboration Agreement is accepted you see the name, the help and the agreement alone; *Before your agreement is accepted*, below, says what to do about that.

What you read about your students comes from the program's records, which this site reads at intervals and not each time you open the page. The line at the foot of the page says when: "Read from the program records on" a date, and how long ago that was. A change an Administrator makes in the program's records can take a while to reach the page.

### Your institution's name

The name sits at the top, with the icon of your institution's own website beside it when that website has one. Under it are the city and country, the website as a link, and "Contact:" with the name of the person the program writes to. They are what the program has on record for your institution. The card **Your institution's details**, in **Representatives and agreement**, is where you correct them: *Your details, your representatives and your agreement* covers it.

### Where to find help

Directly under the name are the places to go with a question:

- **Program updates and announcements** carries what the program publishes for institutions. It says "Nothing new right now." when there is nothing, and **All updates** opens the rest.
- **Resources** holds the Slack logo, which opens the WordPress Credits Slack channel, and **Institution guide**, which opens this guide. It also holds **Need help?** whenever the program has switched its question assistant on for institutions: it answers questions about the program and about WordPress from the WordPress documentation, and links back to the pages its answers came from. Your question is sent to an outside AI service, so do not type anything confidential.
- **Your contact at the program** is under the buttons: the Administrator who looks after institutions in your country, with a link to write to them when the program has an address for them, and **Book a call** when it has a booking link. It is there once the program has set contact details for your country. The page says "They look after institutions in your country. Write to them about anything the guide does not answer."

### Before your agreement is accepted

Until an Administrator has accepted a signed Collaboration Agreement for your institution, or recorded one the program already holds, the section **Collaboration Agreement** is open at the top of the page and the rest of the page is closed. What it says depends on where your agreement stands.

- *None recorded.* It says "The program has no Collaboration Agreement recorded for your institution. Until it does, this page is everything your account can reach." and gives three steps. First, get the agreement: **Generate the agreement** makes the program's own template, filled in with the name you give in the box above it, "The institution's name, exactly as it should print on the agreement", or you upload an agreement of your own, which an Administrator reads in full and so takes longer. The document opens as a page your browser can save as a PDF or print, and nothing is sent to the program until you upload the signed copy. Second, have it signed by somebody who can commit your institution. Third, upload the signed PDF with **Upload the signed agreement**, choosing between "The program's template, signed" and "An agreement of our own, signed", and ticking the box that says the document is signed by somebody who can commit the institution. The file has to be a PDF, and one that is not password protected, since an Administrator opens it. Under the steps the panel adds that an institution that has already signed an agreement with the program does not need to sign again: an Administrator can record the copy the program holds, and your account opens as soon as they do. Write to your contact at the program if that is you.
- *Generated.* The panel says on what day you generated the program's agreement and which template version it was, and that it is not signed and uploaded yet. **Open that document again** gives you the same document, with the name it was made with, and the three steps and the sentence about an agreement already signed stay under it.
- *Waiting for review.* The panel says "We received your signed agreement on" a date, and that an Administrator will review it and you will get an email either way. **Download the copy we hold** gives you the file back, and **Withdraw it** deletes the file from this site so that you can upload another.
- *Returned.* The panel says "An Administrator returned your agreement, with this note:" and shows the note, with a form to upload the corrected agreement, signed, as a PDF.
- *Revoked.* The panel says the program revoked the agreement, that the account reaches this page and nothing else until a new agreement is accepted, and shows the note the program left, with a form to upload a new signed agreement. An Administrator reviews it the same way as the first one.
- *Accepted, but not yet agreed everywhere.* If this site holds an accepted agreement and the program's own record does not agree with it yet, the panel says an Administrator can see the difference and settles it. There is nothing for you to do.

When an Administrator accepts the agreement, everybody at your institution is emailed and the whole page opens. The agreement itself then moves to the foot of **Representatives and agreement**, where *Your details, your representatives and your agreement* picks it up.

### Arranging the page

Each section has two small arrows at its top right, named after it, for example "Move Students up" and "Move Students down". Press one and the section moves at once. The order is your institution's, kept for everyone who signs in for it, and **Students** comes first until somebody moves it.

### Events from our sponsors

Below the three sections, **Events from our sponsors** lists the events the program's sponsors host for its students, mentors, Administrators and institutions, when there are any to join. The heading reads **Events from our sponsors and the program** while an event the program hosts itself is on the list. Each event shows its host, its day and time in your own time zone, how long it runs and how many places are left, with **Join**. Once you are on an event the page says "You are on it.", and shows **Leave**, which gives your place back, and **Join the call** when the event has a call link. The email you get when you join carries the call link and a calendar file.
