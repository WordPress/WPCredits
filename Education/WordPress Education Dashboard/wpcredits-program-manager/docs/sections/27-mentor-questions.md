### Questions from your students

A student whose question **Need help?** could not answer can send it to you. You get an email titled with their first name and "sent you a question from Need help?". It holds the question, a line they added if any, and the guide pages **Need help?** showed them. **Reply to the email to answer**: your reply goes to the student, not to the program.

The question also waits on your Mentor Report Card. A student with a question you have not marked as answered shows a **Question waiting** tag on their row, beside their status, so you see it before you open the row. The tag is not a note and does not change the **Need a call** group.

### The list "Questions sent from Need help?"

Open the student's row. Above their notes a list called **Questions sent from Need help?** shows each question, newest first, with its date, the line the student added and the pages **Need help?** showed. Press **Mark as answered** under a question once you have replied. The tag goes when no question waits. An answered question stays in the list, grayed and dated, for 30 days, and then drops off it.

A student can send up to three questions a day, and not the same question twice in a day. You and the program's Administrators can press **Mark as answered**.

### Need help? notes

**Need help?** may write a note about a student for you to read. It is labeled **Need help?** and shown apart from your own notes, after them. It is not one of your notes: it is not counted among them, does not take the student out of the **Need a call** group and does not move your last-note date. Only Administrators can delete it.
