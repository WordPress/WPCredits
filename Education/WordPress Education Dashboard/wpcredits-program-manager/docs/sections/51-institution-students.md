## Your students

The **Students** section is your roster: the students your institution has on the program, by semester and by where they are, with a list you can download, a card for each student and the form for enrolling more. It opens once your Collaboration Agreement is accepted.

### Reading the roster

At the top of the roster one form sets what you see. **Cohort** is the semester the students started in, each with how many signed up; the roster opens on this semester when you have students in it and on your newest one when you do not. **Group** is **All students** or one of the four groups below. **Search** reads a student's name, WordPress.org username, tutor and field of study; it does not search email addresses. **Show** applies them, and **Clear the filters** takes the group and the search off. The address of a filtered page can be sent to a colleague, who lands on the same list.

Under the form is a summary of the cohort: how many students signed up and how many have graduated, and how that compares with the semester before ("Compared with" the earlier semester and its two numbers, or "No students started in" it).

The students are filed in four groups, each with a count and a line under its name that says what it holds. A group folds and unfolds with one press on its title; **Current** and **Waiting for a mentor** start open, unless a group holds more than twelve students, and **Finished** and **Not on the program now** start closed.

- **Current**: "On the program now, with a mentor assigned or a progress record already started."
- **Waiting for a mentor**: "Signed up, with no mentor assigned and no progress record yet." That is the whole of what the group says. It lists the students the program's records show as signed up with neither, and the page says no more than that.
- **Finished**: "Mentoring has finished. Their details are kept for reference."
- **Not on the program now**: "Students who have not started yet, never began the program, or stopped before finishing it."

A fifth list, **Enrollment not recorded yet**, appears only when students are reporting their progress and the program's records hold no enrollment for them. They are not counted in the groups, and the list says an Administrator needs to complete the record.

Each student is a card. The closed card shows the name, the program they are on, their dates and the name of their mentor when the page has one. For a student who has an account on this site the name is a link that opens the student's own card, described below. For a student who has none the name is not a link and there is no card of their own; their row in the roster still opens like any other. Opened, a card lists **Program**, **Dates**, **Days left**, **Mentor**, **WordPress.org**, **Team**, **Website**, **Field of study**, **Tutor** and **Hours**. A row with nothing in it reads "Not recorded". The **Mentor** row reads "No mentor yet." for a student with none on record. When the program's records name a mentor and the name has not reached the page, the row says so in a sentence of its own instead.

### Opening a student's card

Press a student's name, where it is a link: only a student who has an account on this site has a card. The card opens in place of the roster, with **Back to the roster** at its top, and shows:

- the student's name and program, with their photo when they have a WordPress.org profile picture;
- whether they have signed in yet: for a student who has not, a line says "No sign-in on record since" the day the site began recording sign-ins, and when the last invitation was sent, with **Send invitation** or **Resend invitation**, or, for a student whose account the program has still to confirm, a line saying the Administrators send the invitation once they have confirmed it. The button sends the program's own welcome invitation with a new link, which replaces the link in the earlier email. A student can be sent one invitation every fifteen minutes, and your institution twenty a day;
- a table of **Program**, **Internship duration**, **Cohort**, **Field of study**, **Tutor**, **WordPress.org**, **Contribution teams** and **Personal website**, and then **Mentor**, which reads "No mentor yet" for a student with none on record. **Mentor email** is there only when the student has a mentor, and it is the one email address the card shows; a mentor known by name and not by address keeps the row, and the row reads "Not set";
- **Student Report Card**, a section that is closed until you open it. It is the student's own report, read from the program's records when you open it, so it needs JavaScript, and you cannot change it. A student with no progress record yet has none, and the card says "This student has no progress record yet, so there is no Student Report Card to show.";
- **Correct this student's details**, described below;
- **Download this student's details and course grades as a CSV file**;
- a last line saying how long ago the roster and the student's own details were read.

A student without an account has none of this: no correction form, no **Finishing this placement** and no download of their own. A change for such a student goes through your contact at the program.

### Correcting a student's details

**Correct this student's details** is a closed section under the Student Report Card. Open it and the form says "These five details are yours to correct." They are the **Name on the Student Report Card**, the **Full name**, the **Start date**, the **End date** and the **Field of study**. Everything else on the card is written by the program or by the student, and a change to any of it goes through your contact at the program.

Press **Save changes**. A date is written as YYYY-MM-DD, an end date has to be after the start date, a placement cannot run longer than a year, and the field of study has to be one of the program's own choices; **Not set** clears it. The program's records are written at once and the change is on your roster. The page says "Saved. The change is in the program records and on your roster." and adds a line to your institution's history, which the Administrators read. When a value is refused the form names what was refused and says whether the rest was saved, what you typed and was not saved stays in the form, and the section opens by itself so the sentence is not hidden. If the program's records hold two entries for one student, the dates and the field of study wait until an Administrator has merged them, and the name on the Student Report Card can still be corrected. A student with no progress record yet has no Student Report Card, and so has no correction section.

### Finishing a placement

Under the card, **Finishing this placement** is where you record how a placement ended. The card offers **Mark as dropped out**. **Mark as graduated** is there only if the program switches it on; until it does, an Administrator records a graduation. Either one asks you to confirm first, because it sends the student a letter from the program straight away, and it cannot be undone from this page: an Administrator changes it back in the program's records. The card says why when it offers nothing, for example when the placement is already recorded as finished or the student is paused, and in that case your contact at the program is the one to ask.

### Enrolling students

**Enroll students** is a folded section at the foot of the roster. It is on your page once the program has switched enrollment on for institutions, and it opens by itself when a list is waiting for you or the last attempt has something to say. Nothing is created until you have seen what the list was understood to say.

1. Choose the **Program** the students are joining and the **Start date**, the term they begin; a date more than a year either side of today is refused as a typo. **End date, if you know it** has to be after the start date and at most a year after it. Tick the box that says these students have been notified that their name and email address are shared with the WordPress Foundation for the WordPress Credits Program; it has to be ticked.
2. Add **One student**, with the **Full name**, the **Email address**, the **Field of study** and the **Tutor**, or send **Or a list**: **A CSV file**, or the same text pasted into **Or paste the same thing here**. A list needs a header row and then one student a line. Name and email are needed; field of study and tutor are read when they are there, other common spellings of the headings are read as well, and a column the import does not read is listed back to you and ignored. A list holds up to 300 students, and the file is read and never stored.
3. **Download a sample list**, beside the file box, shows the format. The site makes it when you press: a CSV file with the four headings `name`, `email`, `field_of_study` and `tutor` and one example student. The import reads it as it stands, so you can keep the heading row, replace the example with your own students and send it back. The sample holds nothing about your institution or your students.
4. Press **Check the list**. The page says how many rows can be created and gives every row a sentence: "Ready to create.", "Already on your roster", "Similar name on your roster" (that one is still created), "This student cannot be imported from here. Ask an Administrator.", "The same person as line" another line of the same list (neither is created), or what could not be read in the name or the address.
5. Press **Create these student records** and confirm. The page repeats what is about to happen: how many records, on which program, from which start date. No email is sent when the records are created, and a student receives their login from the program when their account is created. Do not send these students to the registration form; it would create a second record. **Throw this list away** discards a list that has not been created. A long list is created in steps; the page says how many are done and how many are left, and **Continue** picks it up at once if you do not want to wait. When it ends it says how many were created, how many were blocked and how many the program's records refused.

### Downloading lists

**Download this cohort (CSV)**, beside the filters, gives you the whole cohort on the screen as a CSV file, with a column each for the roster group, the student, the program, the start and end dates, the cohort, the mentor, the WordPress.org profile, the team, the website, the field of study, the tutor and the hours. The group and the search narrow the page and leave the file whole. The **Mentor** column is blank for a student with none. A student's card has its own download, **Download this student's details and course grades as a CSV file**, which adds the grades of the student's course.

### What your institution can and cannot see

The roster and the cards show your own students and no one else's. They never show a student's email address, and they never show what a student told the program in confidence about accessibility. Grades appear on a student's card and in that student's own download and nowhere else: they are not on the roster, in a count or in the semester report.

## The semester report

At the end of a term the program drafts a report about the students your institution sent, and an Administrator reviews and approves it. Only then does your institution see it. You read it and download it; you do not write it.

The **Semester report** section is folded, and opens by itself when you follow a link to a report. It lists your institution's reports, one for each semester. An approved report reads "Approved on" a date, with **View** and **Download PDF**. A semester whose report is still being drafted says, for example, "Your semester report for January to June 2026 is being prepared by the program team." With none at all it says "No semester report has been published for your institution yet." Every account of your institution is emailed when a report is approved.

**View** opens the report in the section, with **Back to the other semesters** and **Download PDF**. Its headings are Program Overview, Participation, Contribution Teams, Student Projects and Blogs, Recognition and Events, Continuing Engagement, Student Feedback and Looking Ahead, less any an Administrator has taken out, with figures drawn from your students' records for that term and the words the Administrator wrote around them.

A student is named in it only if that student said you may, and a quote is shown only if the student released it, in the two permission questions at the end of their *Finishing up* feedback form. Those answers are read again each time the report is opened, so a student who changes their mind drops out of the report the next time anyone opens it, even one written weeks ago. When the students' answers cannot be read just now, the page says so and does not show the report.
