## Your Sponsor Dashboard

Your company's page on the Education Directory is the Sponsor Dashboard, at /sponsor-dashboard/. You reach it after signing in with the account the program created for your company; every person the program attached to your company sees the same page. It opens with your company's name, logo and details, then **At a glance**, a row of tiles with your tier and what is waiting for your company, then the program's updates, its resources and your contact at the program, and under them your cards in four sections, **Your sponsorship**, **What you offer**, **Mentors** and **Your company**, in that order until your company arranges them. The header also names the contact person you gave the program, by name alone; the contact address is on the *Company profile and logo* card.

**At a glance.** The first tile is your tier, with the last day of its term and the days left in it, "ending today" on that day, the day the term ended once it has, or "no term"; it is there once the program has launched the sponsor tiers. After it comes one tile for each kind of thing that is waiting for your company, with a number and a line that says what waits: *Offers returned with a note*, *Offers running low on codes* (live offers with fewer codes left than the number you chose for the warning), *Offers not switched on yet*, *Posts returned with a note*, *Event returned*, *Collaboration Agreement returned* or *Collaboration Agreement revoked*, and *Company profile details missing*, which counts how many of the three details are empty. An offer is counted once, under the first of its three lines that fits it, so an offer an Administrator returned is never also counted as not switched on yet. A kind with nothing waiting has no tile, and when nothing at all waits, one dimmed tile says "Nothing waits for you". A tile that waits has a red edge at its start, and its line always says what waits, so the red is never the only sign. The tier tile waits too in the last thirty days of a term and once a term has ended; it always opens the *Sponsorship* card, where the card says how to renew. Press a tile with something waiting and the page takes you to the card that holds it, opened. The *Event returned* tile is there only while the *Events* card can show the note, which stands above the form that plans an event: from Champion up, with no event of yours still to take place, while your sponsorship is not paused and your term still has an event to plan. It is the plan returned in your current term that the note and the tile show: once your term is renewed, a plan dated in the term before is not shown again, even in the days that term still runs. The figures that used to have tiles of their own, your next event, how many offers are live, how many mentors your company sponsors and your yearly reports, are on the lines of their cards.

**Help.** Under the tiles and above the cards, *Program updates and announcements* carries what the program publishes for sponsors, and *Resources* has this guide, the program's Slack channel and the Need help? button. *Your contact at the program* is under those buttons: the Administrator who looks after your company, with a link to write to them when the program has an address for them, and **Book a call** when it has a booking link. The page says "They look after your company's sponsorship. Write to them about anything the guide does not answer." The contact is there once the program has assigned an Administrator to your company, and only while that person still is one.

**Arranging the page.** Each section has two small arrows at its top right, named after it, for example "Move Your sponsorship up" and "Move Your sponsorship down". Press one and the section moves at once. The order is your company's, kept for everyone who signs in for it, and **Your sponsorship** comes first until somebody moves it. A section with nothing to show is left out and has no arrows: until the program has launched the sponsor tiers, **Your sponsorship** is not on the page.

**The cards.** A card folds and unfolds with one press on its title. The number beside a title is how many things it holds, and the words after it say where the card stands, so a folded card still tells you what is in it: "No event planned", "2 of 3 fields filled", "Accepted on September 3, 2026". Where a card has one thing to do, its button sits beside it: *Plan an event*, *New offer*, *Edit*, and *Upload* when the program has sent your agreement back or it is no longer in force, each of which opens the card at its top, the form in it; and *Write a post*, which opens the WordPress editor. A card's button shows while the card is folded; open it and the button gives way to the card. *Sponsorship*, *Offers and codes* and *Mentors you sponsor* are open when you arrive, and so is *Events* while an event of yours is still to take place; every other card stays folded until you open it, and after you send one of a card's forms the page comes back with that card open.

**Your sponsorship.** *Sponsorship* comes first: your tier and, when it has a term, the last day of that term; from Backer up, what your sponsorship funds; what your tier includes, as a checklist; and, below Partner, what the next tier adds. What each tier includes is what the program's partner page says for that level; above Friend, a line over the checklist says it comes with everything in the level below, for example "Everything in Backer, plus:". Its line says your tier with the term's last day and the days left in it. One press on *Talk to your program contact* tells them you would like to talk about the next tier. In the last thirty days of a term the card says how many days are left; from the day after the last day your company reads as a Friend, and the same button then asks your program contact to renew the term. The button keeps what you asked on record. Once the program's Partners page is published, the line with your tier links to it, at every level, since every partner is listed there in one alphabetical list; while a term runs the link follows the term's last day, and once a term has ended the line says so and asks for its renewal without it. At Friend the checklist says your company name is on the partner page. From Champion up, *Events* follows, with the events your tier includes and the planner for your co-hosted event; the paragraph after this one is about it. *Reports* follows once your program contact has finished your first yearly report: your yearly report of the program and of your sponsorship, one for each term - *Open* shows it in a new tab, ready to print, and *PDF* downloads the copy your program contact attached. Its line says how many yearly reports you have and which term the latest is for. *Badge* follows from Supporter up: the badge in its light and dark versions, and the embed code with a Copy button; the badge names your tier and the current year, and once a term has ended the year it ended, and its line says the same. The program's site serves the badge, so a change of tier shows on your Sponsor Dashboard at once and on your own site within an hour, with nothing to paste again.

*Events* lists the events your tier includes and holds the planner for one of them, your co-hosted webinar or event. It is open while an event of yours is still to take place, and its line names the soonest one, its day in your time zone and how many people have joined, or, until the program approves it, that it is waiting for the program; with none, it says "No event planned", and *Plan an event* beside it opens the card, where the form is, whenever you can plan one. The program plans the partners session, and for a Partner the launch communications, with you. You plan one event at a time and, while a term runs, one per term, or as many more as the program grants for that term, dated no later than the term's last day: the date and the start time, in your own time zone, which the form names; the length in minutes; the places, left blank for no limit; a title; a description, which the Partners page shows once the program approves the event, so the call link goes in its own box; and the call link, which only the people who join, and the program, see. A term's days are counted on the site's clock, so a start late on the term's last day in your own time zone can already fall on the next day there; the planner then names the last day and the site's time zone, for example "Choose a start on or before January 31, 2028, the last day of your sponsorship term on the site's clock (Europe / Warsaw)." When your term is renewed before it ends, the current term still runs to its last day and the renewed term starts the day after it; an event dated in the renewed term stays in it, and moving it earlier is refused in the same way, for example "Choose a start on or after February 1, 2027, the first day of your current sponsorship term on the site's clock (Europe / Warsaw)." When the program grants your term more events, the list says so under the co-hosted event, for example "and 2 more this term, granted by the program", and the form says how many your term allows, for example "Your term allows 3 events."; they count for that term alone, and a renewed term starts again from your tier's one. *Plan the event* sends it to your program contact, and nobody else sees it until an Administrator approves it. If the program returns it with a note instead, everyone who can sign in for your company is emailed the note, and it stands above the form when you plan again; once your term is renewed, a note on a plan dated in the term before is no longer shown there, even in the days that term still runs. Once it is approved, everyone who can sign in for your company is emailed a calendar file for it, and the event is listed for the program's students and mentors beside their calls, for Administrators on the Administrator Dashboard and for institution contacts on the Institution Dashboard, where each of them can join it or leave it. The Partners page lists it too, under *Upcoming events*, until it ends: its title, your company's name, its day and time in the site's time zone, its length and its description, with any address in it that starts with http://, https:// or mailto: taken out, and never the call link, the places or who joined; under the list, a visitor who is not signed in finds a *Sign in to join* button, and a student, mentor, institution contact or Administrator who is signed in a link to the page where they join it. Until it has taken place, *Your event* shows where it stands, "Waiting for the program's OK." or approved with how many people have joined, never who, with *Change the event* and *Cancel the event*. When *Plan the event* or *Save the changes* is refused for something you can put right, a detail missing or not valid, a start outside your term or one that has passed, fewer places than the people already on the event, another change to your company's events going through or a save that failed, the form comes back once with what you typed, *Change the event* drawn open when it was a change, and a value longer than its box allows comes back empty. A cancel, or a change of the date, the start time, the length, the title or the call link, emails everyone who joined, with a calendar file that updates the event or takes it off their calendar: the first fifty straight away and the rest within minutes, and the card says whether everyone who joined has been told or is being told. Everyone who can sign in for your company is emailed each change and cancel too, once the event was approved, and your program contact is told; a change of the description or the places alone reaches only them, and saving the form without changing anything emails nobody and says "Nothing changed." The day before your event, everyone who joined is emailed a reminder with the call link, and everyone who can sign in for your company one that says how many people have joined so far, never who; neither carries a calendar file. The reminders go out with the program's daily run, normally the day before the event, sometimes closer to it, and never twice: an event approved less than a day and a half before it starts may get none, and once they have gone out a change sends no second one, since its own email gives the new time. In the form's place the card then says "Your event for this term is planned already." while your term runs, or "Your next event can be planned once this one has taken place." if your sponsorship has no term, or when the program granted your term more events and another can still follow this one; once the event still to take place is the last your term holds, "Your events for this term are planned already."; and once your event for the term has taken place, "Your event for this term has taken place already.", or, once every event the program granted it has, "Your events for this term have taken place already.", for the rest of the term. A plan pressed anyway is refused in the same words. Each person who can sign in for your company has ten presses an hour across *Plan the event*, *Save the changes* and *Cancel the event*; the next one reads "Ten an hour is the limit. Try again in an hour." *Past events* lists the events that took place, each with its day and how many people joined. If your sponsorship stops including events - your term ends, your tier changes to one without them, or the program pauses your sponsorship - the card keeps an event of yours still to take place with *Cancel the event* alone, and an approved one reads "Approved, but no longer offered: your company's sponsorship does not include events at the moment."; nobody new can join it. With nothing still to take place, there is no *Events* card.

**What you offer.** *Offers and codes* is what your company gives the program's students and mentors: its line says how many of your offers are live, how many offers and changes wait for an Administrator's review while any do, and how many times they were claimed this month, and the card opens on the form for a new offer; the next section is about it. *Usage* counts, by month and by offer, how many people took an offer: a chart of the last twelve months, a table and a CSV to download, and its line counts every claim. Nobody is named there. *Posts* lists the posts your company wrote for the program's students and where each one stands; its line counts the published ones and the ones waiting for review, and while the card is folded *Write a post* sits beside it whenever you can write one. Posts start at Backer, so at Friend and Supporter the card is there only while your company has a post of its own, one from before the levels, one returned to you or one the program held back, and then says that your tier includes no posts. A post appears under your offer in Tools from our sponsors, on the Student Report Card and the Mentor Report Card, once an Administrator publishes it; the last section is about posts. To tell the program what more your company would like to do, write to your program contact, named under *Your contact at the program*, near the top of the page.

**Mentors.** *Mentors you sponsor* lists the mentors your company sponsors and how many students each one guides; its line counts them, or says "None yet". *Mentors looking for a sponsor* lists the active mentors who asked to be listed; one press tells your program contact you would like to sponsor one of them.

**Your company.** *Company profile and logo* is your company as the program records it: the details on the left, your logo on the right, and a line saying whether all three details are filled in: the website, the contact person and the contact email, anything else you would like to share being optional. What you save here is kept by the program, so keep it current; a logo in color and one in white for a dark background are shown wherever the program names you. Logos look alike side by side when they share a shape, so upload a horizontal one, about twice as wide as it is tall, at least 300 pixels wide (600 or more looks sharper), as a PNG, JPEG or WebP image, transparent where possible. From Supporter up, the profile also holds a *Description for the partner page*: a sentence or two about your company, 300 characters at most, shown with your logo and your name on the program's Partners page, where every partner is listed in the same format, in alphabetical order. A Friend is listed by name alone, so the box is not there, and a description saved while your company was a Supporter or above is kept but not shown. *People* lists everyone who can sign in for your company; ask your program contact to add or remove a person. *Collaboration Agreement* is where the agreement between your company and the program lives: download the text, upload the signed copy, and read where it stands (waiting for review, accepted, returned with a note, or out of force), which its line says too, with the day it was accepted.
