# Track Builder T5 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Remove the four hand-written report forms and their rows in the program map now that every track runs from its definition on the live site (switched 22 September 2026, the product owner's word on 26 September 2026), keep the lock that lets the four migrated tracks keep their reserved statuses and keys, retire the switch, the refresh and the "Runs from" column, and ship the result as plugin 1.116.0 with nothing a student, a mentor or an institution sees changing.

**Architecture:** No new module and no new screen. `WPCPM_Student_Report_Form::fields()` becomes the compiled tracks' filter alone, with the empty key read as `150h` (decision 34) and an unknown key answered with the 150-hour track's compiled form, as today. `WPCPM_Program`'s five maps start empty and are filled by `WPCPM_Tracks`' filters from the compiled option; the `STATUS_*` constants stay. The four reserved pairs (status to key) move from the hand-written rows into `WPCPM_Tracks::reserved_key()`, which is what `validation_context()` locks a definition of one of those statuses to, so the four tracks compile with their reserved keys and no other track can take them. The store loses the switch both ways, the refresh and the PHP comparison; the seeds stay as what a fresh site seeds from; the screens lose the built-in column, buttons and wording; the History keeps the words for the log kinds a site already holds. An upgrade step compiles once on the first load of 1.116.0 so a site whose compiled option predates the definitions runs on them at once.

**Tech Stack:** WordPress 6.5 and PHP 7.4 as floors, WordPress coding standards, the plugin's standalone `bin/test-*.php` suites, `bin/build-docs.php` for the guides, no build step.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-09-10-track-builder-design.md`: decision 3.5 (the switch as the migration gate; the product owner's word deletes the PHP), decision 34 (a student on no track reads the 150-hour track's form), section 8 (the T5 paragraph: the three branches, `builtin_fields()` and the four rows go; the `STATUS_*` constants stay; the built-in marks are cleared; the suite compares the compiled stores with the seed files), section 10 (`ALUMNI_FIELDS` stays until T5), the T5 row of section 12 (the lock), section 13 item 6 (`locked`). The inventory read for this plan: `.superpowers/sdd/inventory-t5/inv-t5.md` in the plan's workspace.

## What this plan decides

1. **The lock is a map of four pairs, not the PHP.** `WPCPM_Tracks::RESERVED_PAIRS` names the four `WPCPM_Program::STATUS_*` constants against `150h`, `50h`, `dev` and `design`, and `WPCPM_Tracks::reserved_key( $status )` answers the key or `''`. `WPCPM_Track_Store::locked()` (the private helper `context()` calls, `includes/tracks/class-wpcpm-track-store.php:723-746`) locks a definition whose status is one of the four to that pair in both its branches, the compile's and the publishing one, with no mark on the post; `WPCPM_Tracks::validation_context()` (`includes/tracks/class-wpcpm-tracks.php:339-371`) no longer reads the PHP rows and keeps the four statuses and names out of a new track's reach through the reserved rules below. Nothing consults `builtin_key()` or `builtin_row()`, which go.
2. **A reserved status is refused by name.** `WPCPM_Track_Definition::validate_status()` refuses a definition whose status is one of the four unless its key is the pair's, with a new code `status_reserved` and the sentence "This status belongs to one of the program's four original tracks; a new track needs a status of its own." `RESERVED_KEYS` and `key_reserved` stay for the keys. So a new track can take neither the status nor the key of an original track, whatever the seeded posts hold.
3. **The four original tracks always run.** `delete()` refuses a track whose status is reserved ("The program's original tracks are kept: this one is the record of a form the program runs."), in place of the built-in rule at `:1256-1258`; `unpublish()` (and `WPCPM_Track_Publish::take_down()`, `includes/tracks/class-wpcpm-track-publish.php:593-623`) refuses one too, before the student count, since the 150-hour track's form is what every student on no track reads (decision 34) and the other three are the program's base statuses; the screen draws neither Delete nor Unpublish for them. The student guard stays the rule for every other track.
4. **`fields()` is the filter alone.** `WPCPM_Student_Report_Form::fields( $track )` reads `''` as `150h` (decision 34), then `apply_filters( 'wpcpm_report_form_fields', array(), $track )`; `WPCPM_Tracks::filter_fields()` answers the compiled form of a known key and, for a key no live track holds, the 150-hour track's compiled form, which is what `fields()` answered for an unknown key while the hand-written set was the fallback. `builtin_fields()`, its three branches and `ALUMNI_FIELDS` go; `for_institution()` drops `email` questions and every question flagged `hide_from_institution`, and `bin/test-report-form.php` proves the compiled Developer and Designer forms lose the same three questions the constant named.
5. **The five maps start empty and the filters fill them.** `labels()`, `courses()`, `course_ids()`, `hours_targets()` and `track()` in `includes/class-wpcpm-program.php` keep their filters and lose their four rows; the `STATUS_*` constants stay with a docblock line naming what still reads them (`RESERVED_PAIRS`, the syncs, the settings' defaults). `builtin_row()`, `builtin_key()`, `unfiltered()` and `$suspended` leave `WPCPM_Tracks`; `live()` accepts a compiled row with or without `source` (the rows a site holds carry `definition`; `row()` stops writing it), so nothing disappears between the upgrade and the first compile.
6. **The switch, the refresh and the PHP comparison go; the seeds stay and are published at seeding.** `switch_to_definition()`, `switch_to_builtin()`, `refresh_builtins()`, `refresh_builtin()`, `equivalence()`, `php_differences()`, `definition_differences()`, `BUILTIN_KEYS`, `source()`'s `builtin` answer and `META_SOURCE`'s writes leave the store with their handlers (`handle_switch_definition`, `handle_switch_builtin`, `handle_refresh`), buttons, the "Runs from" column, "Publish definition", the read-only editor, the stale-draft preview line, `stale()` and the preflight's `builtin_changed` refusal; `META_SWITCHED` stays as the record of the migration; the History screen keeps its labels for the `switch_definition` and `switch_builtin` log kinds a site already holds. `seed()` creates each of the four tracks published (the definition and the published copy both the seed, a `publish` row in the log naming the plugin), then compiles, so a fresh site runs its four tracks at once as it ran their PHP; the preflight is not asked, because the seeds are the record of the forms the base already held the columns for. `bin/build-seeds.php` goes; `bin/seed-definitions.php` keeps only the functions that read no PHP; `bin/test-track-definitions.php` pins each seed file's md5, its status and key against `RESERVED_PAIRS`, that every rule accepts it under the lock, and that its compiled fields carry the question counts 24, 11, 31 and 49.
7. **The upgrade step lives where the once-per-site step already lives.** `WPCPM_Track_Store::SEED_VERSION` becomes 2; `maybe_seed()`'s older-stamp branch (`:766-769`, on `init` 20 after the post type) no longer refreshes drafts and instead, once: publishes any track post still marked `builtin` from its definition (a built-in draft on a site that never switched) and deletes the `_wpcpm_track_source` meta from every track post, then compiles, so a site on 1.110.4 whose rows are current recompiles to the same rows. No new option; nothing for `uninstall.php`.
8. **The guides say the four tracks are the program's original tracks, run from their definitions.** The "four built-in tracks" subsection of `docs/sections/32-admin-tools.md` becomes the account of what a fresh site seeds and publishes, the lock, and why they cannot be deleted or unpublished; the list table loses its "Runs from" row; every sentence naming the switch, the refresh or "Publish definition" goes or is reworded; the spec's T5 row and section 8 record the removal and decisions 1 to 3 and 6 above as decisions 36 to 39; the readme entry names the removal in one paragraph.
9. **`docs/images/track-builder-list.png` is not retaken here**: it shows the old "Runs from" column; a screenshot needs a signed-in browser and waits for the docs-only release the owner planned for screenshots.
